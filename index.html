Research

What I work on

I study generative models of how the physical world moves: how to make that motion controllable, and how to use it to teach robots.

01

Controllable video generation

Steering large video diffusion models with camera paths and object trajectories, without adding control branches.

02

World models

Action-conditioned video models that imagine how a scene evolves as a robot acts in it.

03

Robot learning

Turning imagined rollouts into training data for vision-language-action policies such as π0.5.

UniCaMo: Unified Camera and Motion Control for Video Diffusion Models

Long Vu*, Tan Ngo*, Animesh Karnewar, Amir Habibian, Binh-Son Hua, Hung Bui, Minh Hoai Nguyen, Phong Nguyen-Ha (* equal contribution)

Controls object trajectories and camera motion together by constructing the initial noise of a video diffusion model in 3D. No adapters or architecture changes, only LoRA fine-tuning of Wan 2.1 14B. State of the art on MoveBench in both video quality and motion accuracy.

Ongoing·Qualcomm AI Research

RoboDream: High-Quality Data Generation for Robotic Manipulation

Small robot datasets limit what a policy can learn. I built RoboDream's end-to-end pipeline: it fine-tunes an action-conditioned world model, rolls out a policy inside it, filters the imagined episodes, and trains vision-language-action policies on the result. Adding the synthesized data raises π0.5 pick-and-place success from 17.9% to 23.1% in simulation, and task success from 45% to 55% on a real robot.

IQAGA: Image Quality Assessment-Driven Learning with GAN-Based Dataset Augmentation for Cross-Domain Person Re-Identification

Dang H. Pham, Anh D. Nguyen, Long V. Vu, Hoa N. Nguyen

StarGAN-based augmentation with a quality-aware loss that down-weights poor generated images, improving cross-domain re-identification by 2.3 mAP and 3.4 Rank-1.
